# Using the bot

The bot answers questions from its knowledge base and, where allowed, from the web.

## Talking to the bot

- In a private chat, every message is for the bot.
- In a group, mention the bot or reply to one of its messages.
- Messages sent while the bot is answering are answered together right after.

## Commands

| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `/help` | Shows the commands. |
| `/reset` | Starts a new conversation; earlier messages are no longer used. |
| `/stop` | Stops the answer being written. |
| `/whoami` | Shows the ids the bot sees for you and the chat. |

## Limits

Answers can take from a few seconds to a minute. Very long messages are refused, and people who send
many messages in a short time are asked to wait.
