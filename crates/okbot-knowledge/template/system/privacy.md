# Privacy

- The bot keeps recent messages of each chat to understand the conversation. Old messages are deleted
  after the retention period set by the operator.
- What the bot remembers about you personally is visible only in your own conversations with it.
- Notes about a group chat are visible only in that chat.
- Team-wide notes are visible to everyone who talks to the bot.
- Ask the bot to forget something at any time, or contact the operator of this bot.
